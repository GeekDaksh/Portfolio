01 — About

I'm Daksh — an AI/ML engineer in my final year at NMIMS MPSTME. I like problems where the data is messy, the stakes are real, and a wrong answer costs something.

Research. My pan-cancer classification paper is published at IEEE ICBCB 2026 (presented in Kitakyushu, Japan). My uncertainty-aware multi-agent battery charging work is accepted at INDICON 2026.

Recent builds. I completed a Self-Driving Lab for concrete mix design as my capstone — Bayesian optimisation on top of a code-checked engineering engine. I've also built multimodal media AI (MediaLens) and ballistics simulation at L&T. Today I write STM32 firmware as a Firmware Intern at Meshnet Electronics.

How I work. Every pipeline step gets a check. Every metric has to be explainable. Then I try to break what I built, and rebuild it stronger. My daily stack: Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, LightGBM, BoTorch/Ax, LangGraph, FAISS, C/C++ — with AI coding tools like Claude Code to move faster.

Self-Driving Lab for Concrete Mix Design
◆ Final-Year Capstone · Completed
An AI lab assistant that designs IS-code-compliant concrete mixes (M10–M65) using manufactured sand. A tested IS 10262 engine does every calculation; LightGBM and a Gaussian Process predict strength and workability with uncertainty; a qNEHVI Bayesian optimiser ranks mixes on strength, workability and cost. Durability is a hard gate, and every number traces back to an IS clause.
